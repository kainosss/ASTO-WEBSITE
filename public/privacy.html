Privacy notice

This notice explains how ASTO Security handles the details you share through this website. ASTO Security is a Brisbane-based electronic security business offering CCTV, intruder alarms and preventative maintenance.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number (if you provide it), suburb, property type, the service you are interested in and your message. Please do not include alarm codes, passwords or other sensitive security information in an enquiry.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, to discuss your property and needs, to prepare a quote and to arrange any work you ask us to carry out. We do not sell your details or add you to marketing lists.

Who else handles your details

  • Website and enquiry form: this website and its enquiry form are run on Cloudflare, which processes your enquiry and technical information such as your IP address to deliver the site and pass your enquiry to us. We do not store enquiries on the website itself.
  • Spam protection: the enquiry form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks technical signals from your browser and connection to confirm that the form is being used by a person.
  • Email: your enquiry is emailed to our business inbox, which is provided by Google Workspace. Cloudflare and Google may process and store information outside Australia, including in the United States.
  • Fonts: the website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when the page loads.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Cloudflare Turnstile may use technical data needed for the spam check.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to respond to you, to carry out any work you ask us to do and to meet our record-keeping obligations. We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure.

Accessing or correcting your details

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Please email admin@astosec.com or use the enquiry form.

Concerns

If you have a concern about how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
