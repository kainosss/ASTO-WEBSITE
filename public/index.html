A few common
questions.

Not sure what your property needs? A quick conversation is often the best place to start.

Can you help with an existing camera or alarm system?

Existing systems may be suitable for troubleshooting, servicing or targeted upgrades. What’s possible will depend on the equipment, its condition and compatibility.

Do you work on both homes and businesses?

ASTO is focused on residential properties and smaller commercial premises such as shops, small offices and local workshops around Brisbane.

Do I need a completely new system?

Not necessarily. Sometimes maintenance or a selective upgrade is all that's needed. We can discuss the existing system before considering replacement.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Just tell us your suburb, the type of property, and what you need help with. If you already have a system, its make and a few photos can be useful.
